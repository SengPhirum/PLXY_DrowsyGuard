    Fleet alerting over MQTT

    Every confirmed alert is published as one JSON message. The speaker, the event log and the SD-card captures are unaffected by anything on this screen — if the broker is unreachable, alerts still sound.

    broker.emqx.io is a public test broker. It is preconfigured here so a demonstration works in a room with no broker in it, and it is demonstration and testing only: there is no authentication and no isolation, so anyone who subscribes to the topic — which they can guess — reads every alert this device publishes, including the driver remark. Point it at your own broker before any real vehicle.
    With verification off the connection is encrypted but unauthenticated: anything on the path can present itself as your broker. Use it to get a self-signed broker working, then paste that broker's CA below and turn it back on.

    Driver and device

    The remark is the only field that says who is being monitored. It is deliberately not part of any topic: a driver’s name has no business in a broker’s subscription tree.
